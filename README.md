# hypeclip
HypeClip 🚀

Turn streams into viral clips.

HypeClip is an AI-powered gaming clipping platform designed to transform long gaming streams and recordings into engaging short-form videos.

✨ Features

* 🎮 Twitch, YouTube and Kick support (planned integrations)
* ✂️ Manual video clipping
* 🤖 AI-powered highlight detection (in development)
* 📝 Automatic captions and subtitles
* 📱 Vertical 9:16 video exports
* 🎬 Gaming-focused editing templates
* 🆓 Free plan with 3 clips per day
* 💎 Pro and Creator subscription plans (planned)

🛠️ Technology

* Frontend: React / Vite
* Backend: Node.js / FastAPI (depending on the module)
* Database: Bolt Database
* Video processing: FFmpeg
* AI: Gemini and open-source models

💰 Pricing

* Free: 3 clips per day
* Pro: $5/month
* Creator: $12/month

Proposed pricing; subject to change.

🚧 Development Status

HypeClip is currently under active development.

Our priorities are real video processing, AI highlight detection, user authentication and platform integrations.

🔒 Content and permissions

HypeClip is intended for user-owned or authorized video content. Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to process and publish clips.
